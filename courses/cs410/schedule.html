Homework 1 — ER/EER and the Relational Model

90 points

Model an automobile repair shop as an ER diagram, translate it to relations with keys and integrity constraints, populate it with sample data, then build a separate EER model using generalization. Covers Ch 3–5 and 9.

Released Mon Aug 31, 2026 Due Mon Sep 14, 2026 · 11:59 PM Submit via Canvas
