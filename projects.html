Keith and Catherine Stein Luminary

What it is: An all-digital immersive museum space at Boise State University with touch-activated glass walls, 26K-lumen projectors, and 7.1 digital surround sound. It combines touch-activated screens with immersive projection to present interactive and sensory experiences, with a focus on archiving and presenting cultural histories.

My role: Software architect. I brought to the project the visualization facility leadership experience gained running supercomputer and VR laboratories at KAUST, the San Diego Supercomputer Center, Calit2, and Walt Disney Feature Animation.

OpenPipe

What it is: An open source, federated, cloud-based content management and production pipeline for the digital arts. It federates multiple online digital museums, runs across multiple cloud services, and coordinates distributed production with integrated human and compute operators following a production plan that updates itself as work is done.

My role: Project lead and principal developer, with Rezvan Joshaghani, at Boise State University, 2019 to present.

Stack: AWS, Angular, Python Lambdas, MySQL, a graph database, a REST API, and CloudFront. Cloud-provider-agnostic deployment with an external open interface API.

Outcome: Powers the Stein Luminary's digital art curation system, providing federated search across four museum collections: the Met, the Cleveland Museum of Art, the Rijksmuseum, and the Smithsonian.

Learn more about the OpenPipe project

Blockchain Transaction Visualization

What it is: Interactive visual analytics for cryptocurrency transaction networks. The Ethereum work uses deck.gl to render transaction flows, aggregation density, network graphs, and time series from shared datasets, with node positions and communities derived at load time. The WashDB pipeline collects NFT traces, builds a linking graph, and clusters it with disjoint sets to surface wash trading. Related Bitcoin work analyzed many-to-many transactions, mixing services, and behavioral outliers.

My role: Faculty mentor for the NSF Blockchain Research Experience for Undergraduates site at Boise State, supervising the student teams that built the visualizations and analysis pipeline.

Stack: deck.gl and JavaScript in the browser, CSV transaction and NFT datasets, and a graph-clustering pipeline.

Outcome: Seven papers, posters, and preprints from 2021 to 2026, including Diffusion: Analysis of Many-to-Many Transactions in Bitcoin (IEEE Blockchain 2021), Towards Deanonymization of Mixing Services in Bitcoin (IEEE iGETblockchain 2022), and Detecting Wash Trading in Cryptocurrency (2026).

Privacy Investigation Pipeline

What it is: An automated pipeline for inspecting how websites track their visitors. It examines cookies, cross-site tracking, canvas fingerprinting, consent popups, and session recorders, pixels, and keyloggers, and produces a per-site investigation report. The published documentation covers tracking definitions, an investigation checklist, a notable-cookies reference, and a finding on the opt-out paradox.
